Meet Amanda

Founder & instructor Amanda Cavadas

Amanda is a certified Pilates instructor who started Sea Sculpt Club to make Pilates feel more accessible and welcoming for everyone, bringing together two of her greatest loves: Pilates and the beach.

Every class is designed to feel welcoming for first-timers while still offering a meaningful challenge for regulars.
