Good to Know

Quick FAQs

What should I bring?

Bring your own mat.

Are classes beginner friendly?

Yes. Our mat classes are all-levels, so you’re welcome whether it’s your first class or your fiftieth.

How do I use a class pack?

Buy packs on our Buy page. After checkout, we email you a class code with your classes on it. To book, add any class to your cart and enter the code at checkout. You can use it for friends too, and your classes never expire. Each class is a $35 value, so for premium events that cost more, like Santa Sculpt, you just pay the difference at checkout.
