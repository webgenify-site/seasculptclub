Liability Waiver & Release

Version: October 27, 2026

Please read this carefully. When you book a Sea Sculpt Club class, event, private session or party, you agree to this waiver for yourself and for everyone you book for.

1. Voluntary participation and assumption of risk

I am choosing to take part in Pilates, sculpt, strength, stretching and related movement classes and events offered by Sea Sculpt Club. I understand that physical exercise carries risks, including muscle strains, sprains, falls, joint injuries, overexertion, heart or breathing problems and, in rare cases, serious injury or death. Classes may take place outdoors (including on beaches, sand, grass, pool decks and docks) or at hotels, restaurants, homes, studios and other venues, where uneven surfaces, sun, heat, cold, wind, water and other conditions add risk. I knowingly accept all of these risks, whether known or unknown.

2. My health and fitness

I confirm that I am physically able to take part, and that I have consulted, or have had the opportunity to consult, a physician before starting any exercise program. I will tell the instructor before class about any injury, health condition, pregnancy or limitation, and I am responsible for modifying or stopping any exercise that does not feel right for me. I understand the instructor is not a medical provider and that any guidance offered is not medical advice.

3. Release of liability

To the fullest extent allowed by law, I release, waive and discharge Sea Sculpt Club, its owner, instructors, employees, contractors and volunteers, and the venues and partners that host or co-host its classes and events (together, the “Released Parties”), from any and all claims, demands, losses or liability for injury, illness, death, or loss of or damage to property arising from or related to my participation, including claims caused by the ordinary negligence of any Released Party. This release does not apply to gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

4. Indemnity

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any claims, costs or expenses (including reasonable attorney’s fees) brought by me or on my behalf, or by anyone I booked for, arising from our participation.

5. Medical treatment

If I am injured or become ill and cannot consent, I authorize the instructor or venue staff to call emergency services and arrange first aid or medical treatment. I am responsible for any resulting costs.

6. Personal property

I am responsible for my own belongings, including mats, bags, phones and valuables. The Released Parties are not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged property.

7. Venue rules

I will follow the instructor’s directions and the rules of each venue. Sea Sculpt Club may refuse or end participation for anyone whose condition or conduct puts themselves or others at risk.

8. Booking for others and minors

If I book for other people, I confirm that I have their permission, that I will share this waiver with them, and that they agree to it. Anyone under 18 may only take part with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who agrees to this waiver on the minor’s behalf.

9. Photos and videos

Photos and videos may be taken during classes and events. At checkout you choose whether you are comfortable appearing in photos and videos shared on Sea Sculpt Club’s website and social media; you can change your choice at any time by emailing seasculptclub@gmail.com.

10. General

This waiver is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey. If any part of it is found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. It applies to every class, event and session I book with Sea Sculpt Club, now and in the future, unless replaced by a newer version.

Acknowledgment

By checking “I agree to the Liability Waiver & Release” at checkout, I confirm that I have read and understood this waiver, that I am at least 18 years old, and that I agree to it voluntarily. My agreement is recorded with the date and time of my booking.
