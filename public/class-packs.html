How it works

Three easy steps

  1. Buy your pack. Check out with card, Apple Pay, PayPal or Venmo.
  2. Get your class code. We email it right away. You can also see it, and how many classes are left, under My Account.
  3. Book any class. Add a class to your cart and enter your code at checkout. Bringing a friend? Use your code for their spot too.
Do class packs expire?

No. Your classes stay on your code until you use them.

Can I share my pack?

Yes. Use your code to book spots for friends, or book several spots in one checkout.

Can I use a pack for special events?

Yes. Each class covers any class or event up to $39. For elevated events, you pay the difference at checkout.
